# Eunoia

> Words worth keeping

Eunoia is a personal vocabulary and literature-inspired app designed to help users discover, understand, remember, and use new words.

The idea is simple: introduce a new word every day, make it memorable through context and examples, and let users build their own collection of words over time.

## ✨ The Idea

Eunoia aims to make vocabulary building feel less like studying and more like discovering something worth keeping.

Potential features include:

* A new word every day
* Definitions and pronunciation
* Examples from literature and everyday language
* Word origins and etymology
* Save words to a personal collection
* Review previously discovered words
* Personal notes and examples
* Progress and streaks
* Context-based discovery
* Carefully designed engagement loops to encourage consistent learning

## 🎯 Goal

Build a vocabulary companion that makes learning new words enjoyable, memorable, and something users look forward to every day.

## 🚧 Status

Early concept — development has not started yet.

## 🧠 Name

**Eunoia** comes from the Greek word *eunoia*, associated with beautiful thinking and a well-disposed mind.

The name reflects the larger idea behind the app: discovering better words can lead to richer expression and, ultimately, richer thought.
